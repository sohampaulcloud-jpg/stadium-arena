Controls

Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys to drive and turn. Hold Space to fire while armed. Grab a glowing weapon pickup to equip it.

Weapons last 12 seconds. You can carry only one at a time; you cannot pick up another until it expires or is used up by the timer.

Buttons also work on touch screens.
